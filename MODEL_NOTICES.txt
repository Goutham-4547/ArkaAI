MODEL / RUNTIME NOTES
=====================

Primary Snapdragon runtime:
- Qualcomm GenieX (local runtime)
- Default quality model: ai-hub-models/Qwen3-8B when the detected memory budget allows it.
- Default fast model: ai-hub-models/Qwen3-4B.
- Fallback: ai-hub-models/Qwen3-4B.

Semantic retrieval:
- sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0 model; quantized ONNX asset).

Emotion signal:
- SamLowe/roberta-base-go_emotions-onnx. It is a probabilistic emotion signal,
  not a medical diagnosis.

ArkaAI does not claim ownership of third-party models or runtimes. Check the
specific license/usage terms of any model that you plug in before redistribution.
